# Account identities and invitation email

## Account model

A Navigate profile can have one primary email and multiple secondary authentication identities. Every linked email opens the same roles, dashboard, sessions, surveys, Portfolio, advising shares, and audit history.

The primary email labels the account in administrative views. Secondary emails are delivery and sign-in alternatives. They do not create additional Navigate profiles.

Existing separate accounts may be merged only by the Platform Creator. The merge is blocked when both identities contain responses for the same survey wave so evaluation records can be reviewed first.

## Invitation template

Supabase requires custom SMTP before it allows project-specific email subjects and bodies. After SMTP is connected, configure the **Invite user** template as follows.

**Subject**

`Activate Your Navigate the Pathway Account`

**Body**

```html
<h2>Welcome to Navigate the Pathway</h2>
<p>You have been invited to activate your Navigate the Pathway account.</p>
<p><strong>Account type:</strong> {{ .Data.account_role }}</p>
<p><strong>Primary email:</strong> {{ .Data.primary_email }}</p>
<p>Navigate the Pathway helps students organize their preparation, document meaningful experiences, participate in their cohort, and prepare for advising and application milestones.</p>
<p><a href="{{ .ConfirmationURL }}">Activate your Navigate the Pathway account</a></p>
<p>If you received this invitation at a secondary email address, it will open the same Navigate profile as the primary address shown above.</p>
<p>If you were not expecting this invitation, you may ignore this message.</p>
```

Invitation metadata is supplied server-side. The template must not expose roles, addresses, or links in application source or logs beyond the recipient-specific email generated by Supabase.

## Delivery

The Supabase default sender is appropriate only for early testing. A Roseman-approved SMTP provider and sender domain should be configured before broader invitations. Domain authentication and institutional allowlisting may be needed for reliable delivery to Roseman addresses.
