# Navigate the Pathway planning package

This folder contains the integrated v0.2 specification for a pre-admission Navigate experience for premed juniors and seniors.

Start with:

1. [Product and Software Specification](PRODUCT_AND_SOFTWARE_SPECIFICATION.md) - the integrated v0.2 product vision, startup explainer, first-login branching, ongoing Portfolio, continuity boundary, functional requirements, data model, privacy, accessibility, evaluation, and phased delivery.
2. [First-session Presentation and Playtest](FIRST_SESSION_PRESENTATION_AND_PLAYTEST.md) - the low-instruction student launch, facilitator script, observation guide, debrief, and go/no-go criteria.
3. [Spec Addendum v0.2](SPEC_ADDENDUM_V0.2.md) - the original merge source and implementation prompts preserved for handoff to the prototype codebase.
4. [Prototype v0.2 Handoff](PROTOTYPE_V0.2_HANDOFF.md) - where the current working prototype lives, how to run it, and what to include in a second-pair-of-eyes review.
5. [PI Student and Advisor Overview](PI_STUDENT_ADVISOR_OVERVIEW.md) - the recommended plain-language handout, with two simple diagrams and a ready-to-send introductory blurb.
6. [Detailed Architecture Views](PI_ARCHITECTURE_VIEWS.md) - optional technical appendix covering student, advisor, evaluation-data, longitudinal-continuity, and PI decision views.

## Important scope decision

The medical-school Levels Planner is not a source for this product. Navigate the Pathway serves students before admission. The Roseman College of Medicine admissions guide and current official admissions information govern the pre-admission structure. A future Continuity Bridge may carry explicitly consented student-owned artifacts into a separately governed matriculated-student product, but it is a Phase 3+ item rather than part of the MVP.

## Recommended next workshop

Use a 90-minute stakeholder session to decide:

- initial cohort eligibility;
- identity/SSO model;
- admissions-content owner;
- advisor visibility defaults;
- community moderation owner and coverage;
- whether students under 18 are in scope;
- whether the first release supports Roseman only or multiple target schools; and
- whether any AI assistance is permitted in Story Studio.

Also confirm whether the startup explainer gates the first-login adventure, whether the proposed Portfolio phase labels work for students, who owns any future matriculated-side continuity product, and whether a Research/Evaluation Coordinator role plus separately governed instrument-data schema are approved.

Then create a phone-width clickable prototype of the first-login adventure before estimating the complete platform.
